The construction — weight grows inward, never outward

Both arcs sit on one outer radius, so the silhouette is a single unbroken circle and the entire weight change lands on the inside. Aligning the centrelines instead — the obvious construction — steps the outer edge at both junctions and the mark stops reading as a ring at all.

The caps are butt, not round. On a circular arc a butt cap is perpendicular to the tangent and therefore exactly radial, so both caps meet on the same radius line and the step reads as a crisp shoulder. Round caps bulge past each junction on both arcs and stack into lumps.

Why this one

It is the product's own geometry. Every nutrient row in the app draws a heavy fill against a light track, and the hairline in this ring is that track. So the mark is not a metaphor bolted on afterwards — it is the interface's smallest unit, closed into a circle. It reads as a dial at a glance, survives one colour and 16px, and quietly states the thing the app is for: some of this is known, some of it is not, and the difference is visible.
