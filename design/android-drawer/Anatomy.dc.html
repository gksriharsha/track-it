The tax, and what pays it

A drawer costs a reach to the top-left corner plus a tap on every switch, and that lands on Today too. I argued for keeping three shortcuts on the bottom edge to avoid it; that version is on the second page. It was not chosen, and the instruction it contradicted was the plain one: the menu comes off the bottom.

The drawer earns its place a different way: eleven destinations, not five. The six that could not fit were behind a Library hub you had to walk through, and the drawer holds that list instead — so a screen leaves the app and Recipes is one tap from anywhere.
