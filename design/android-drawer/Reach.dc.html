What the zones decide
Hourly goes on the edge. Today, Nutrients and History are what a food diary is opened for. Behind a hamburger they would each cost a reach to the worst corner plus a tap, every meal.
Weekly can be hard to reach. Vessel weights and a supplement panel are set up once and edited rarely. Paying a stretch for those is the right trade — and it is what frees the edge.
Three beats five. Dropping from five slots to three widens each target from 78 to 130 pt. The bar got more thumb-friendly by holding less, which is the part that is easy to miss.
The count that settles it
11 destinations · 5 bar slots

The six that would not fit were behind a Library hub — a screen whose only job was to hold a list. The drawer holds that list instead, so Recipes is one tap from anywhere rather than three, and a screen disappears from the app.

The drawer still lists all eleven, the three shortcuts included. A menu that omitted them would be an overflow bin; listing them makes it the map, with the bar as the shortcut.
