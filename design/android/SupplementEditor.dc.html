Which panel is on it

A US panel must declare fifteen nutrients whenever they are present above a threshold — and must not declare them below it. So leaving one of those out is the pack saying “less than that”, and this app counts it as a bound. Everything else is voluntary: its absence says nothing.

This panel lists everything in the product. Your own judgement, not the regulation's — a nutrient can be present from an undeclared route. Ticking it counts every omission as a real zero instead of an unknown.
